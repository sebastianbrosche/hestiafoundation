End grain wood floors: "But that takes forever, right?"

Yes, it is slow. That is exactly why it is cheap. A end grain floor made from reclaimed timber costs a fraction of engineered hardwood and outlasts it by decades. The catch: you cut the blocks yourself.

Blown-in rockwool: "But isn't that the itchy stuff?"

Rockwool has a reputation. It is also the only insulation that is fireproof, hydrophobic, and breathable all at once. Blown-in installation means no cutting, no gaps, and a wall that actually performs to its R-value.

Composting toilets: "But won't it smell?"

A properly vented composting toilet smells less than a flush toilet. The air is sucked downward through the seat, creating negative pressure. The bathroom smells better after you use it than before.
