# A Low Cost High Tech Grid Independent Luxury Home from Discarded Shipping Pallets, You Can Build Yourself in 2 Weeks for Under 50k

*Yes, we know how that sounds. Read on.*

## The Setup

Europe produces 500 million standardized timber pallets every year. They are built to ISO standards, treated for weather resistance, and designed to carry 2,000 kg across continents. Then they are thrown away.

We did not invent the pallet. We noticed that nobody had written down how to turn it into a house. So we did. And published it for free.

## The Numbers That Make People Angry

| What the market says | What the math says |
|---|---|
| Average house in Portugal: €290,000 | Pallet shell: €18,500 |
| Time to build: 12-18 months | Time to build: 10 days |
| Contractors needed: 15-20 | People needed: 4 |
| Tools needed: industrial | Tools needed: drill, saw, level, hammer |
| Grid connection: mandatory | Solar + battery: included |
| Permits: 6-12 months | Permits: same, but the house is reversible |

## Why This Is Not a Shack

- **Thermal performance:** U-value 0.22 W/m²K. Better than most existing housing stock.
- **Lifespan:** 50+ years with standard maintenance. The frame is wood. Wood lasts.
- **Finish:** Interior cladding, exterior render, proper windows, proper roof. It looks like a house because it is a house.
- **Expandable:** Start with 37m². Add a module. Add another. The grid grows with you.

## The Rage

The housing industry does not want this to exist. It breaks every assumption:
- That homes must be expensive.
- That homes must be built by professionals.
- That homes must be permanent to be valuable.
- That the grid is something you plug into, not something you own.

A reversible, modular, off-grid house built from waste material in two weeks undermines the entire supply chain. No mortgage broker required. No developer. No 30-year debt.

## The Truth

This is not a fantasy. We are building the first prototype in Lagos, Portugal, in 2026. The drawings are public. The cost breakdown is public. The assembly manual is public.

If you are angry reading this, good. That means the numbers are working.
